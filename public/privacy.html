Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Jungleboat Vacations.

Your privacy is important to us. Jungleboat Vacations is committed to respecting and protecting the personal information you provide when visiting our website, requesting travel-planning assistance, or communicating with us.

Jungleboat Vacations provides personalized travel-planning services specializing in Walt Disney World, Disneyland, Disney Cruise Line, and other travel experiences.

Jungleboat Vacations operates as an independent travel advisor affiliated with Best Day Ever Vacations.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

When you visit our website, complete a travel-planning inquiry form, respond to an advertisement, or contact us directly, we may collect the following information:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Travel destinations of interest
  • Preferred travel dates
  • Number of travelers and ages of children
  • Resort, cruise, and accommodation preferences
  • Estimated travel budget
  • Special requests or other information you voluntarily provide

We may also collect limited technical information, including your IP address, browser type, device information, and website usage data through analytics and advertising technologies.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information collected by Jungleboat Vacations may be used to:

  • Respond to your travel inquiries.
  • Provide personalized travel recommendations.
  • Prepare travel quotes and itineraries.
  • Assist with resort, cruise, and travel reservations.
  • Communicate regarding existing or potential travel arrangements.
  • Provide customer service and planning assistance.
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Measure the effectiveness of our advertising.
  • Send promotional communications when you have consented to receive them.

We do not sell your personal contact information to third-party marketing companies.

4. Sharing Information With Travel Partners

Jungleboat Vacations operates as an independent travel advisor affiliated with Best Day Ever Vacations.

When necessary to provide requested travel services, we may share relevant information with:

  • Best Day Ever Vacations
  • Walt Disney Travel Company and Disney travel-related services
  • Disney Cruise Line
  • Other cruise lines and travel suppliers
  • Hotels, resorts, and transportation providers
  • Authorized travel booking and itinerary management platforms

Information is shared only as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, prepare quotes, arrange reservations, or provide requested travel services.

Third-party travel providers may have their own privacy policies governing how they process personal information.

5. Google Ads and Online Advertising

Jungleboat Vacations may advertise its services through Google Ads and other online advertising platforms.

When you submit an inquiry through a Google Ads lead form, we may receive information you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, telephone number, and responses to any questions included in the form.

We use this information to respond to your inquiry, discuss travel-planning options, and provide requested services.

We may also use Google Analytics and other advertising measurement technologies to understand website traffic and evaluate advertising performance.

Google’s collection and processing of information are governed by Google’s applicable privacy policies.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, understand visitor activity, and evaluate advertising effectiveness.

These technologies may collect information such as pages visited, browser type, approximate geographic location, and interactions with our website.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings and, where available, through cookie preference controls on our website.

7. Communication With Customers

By submitting an inquiry through our website or advertising forms, you authorize Jungleboat Vacations to contact you regarding your request using the contact information you provide.

Communications may include email or telephone contact to discuss travel preferences, pricing, availability, and planning assistance.

Submitting an inquiry does not automatically enroll you in recurring promotional emails or text messages.

You may request that we stop contacting you at any time.

8. Information Security

Jungleboat Vacations takes reasonable administrative and technical precautions to protect personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

We encourage customers not to submit sensitive payment information, passwords, or government identification numbers through general website inquiry forms.

When payment information is required for travel reservations, customers will be directed to an appropriate authorized payment process.

9. Information Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill travel-planning requests, provide services, maintain appropriate business records, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

Customers may request deletion of their personal information, subject to applicable legal, contractual, or recordkeeping requirements.

10. Children’s Privacy

Jungleboat Vacations provides travel-planning services to adults arranging travel for themselves and their families.

Our website and advertising are not intended to collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13.

Information about children traveling with a family may be collected from a parent or legal guardian when necessary to prepare travel arrangements.

11. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact Jungleboat Vacations to:

  • Request access to personal information we maintain about you.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information, where applicable.
  • Opt out of promotional communications.
  • Ask questions about how your information is used.

We will respond to privacy-related requests in accordance with applicable laws.

12. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to Disney, cruise lines, resorts, travel suppliers, and other third-party websites.

Jungleboat Vacations is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of independently operated websites.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of third-party websites before submitting personal information.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Jungleboat Vacations may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in business operations, technology, advertising practices, or legal requirements.

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
